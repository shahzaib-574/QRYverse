Data controls

Delete your local QRYverse data.

The v1 Play release is intended only for adults ages 18 and older. It creates no QRYverse account and sends no content to QRYverse Cloud. You control the data stored on this device and any copies you exported.

Remove data from this device

Deleting local data does not delete copies you exported to another destination.

  1. Delete saved QR items from Library and remove Track workspaces or records you no longer need.
  2. In Studio → Privacy & diagnostics, erase the local diagnostic history.
  3. For a complete local reset, open Android Settings → Apps → QRYverse → Storage & cache → Clear storage. Uninstalling QRYverse also removes its private app storage because Android backup is disabled for this app.
  4. Delete exported files separately from the folder, cloud drive, printer service, messaging app, or other share destination where you placed them.

A browser installation stores data in that browser's site storage. Use the browser's site-data controls for the QRYverse origin to remove it.

No QRYverse account in this release

There is no QRYverse account or first-party cloud record to request deletion from in v1. If a future release adds accounts or hosted services, this page and the in-app controls will be updated before that feature becomes available.

Third parties

QRYverse does not offer subscriptions in this release. Google AdMob, export destinations, share targets, webhook services, and websites opened from QR codes maintain their own records; contact those providers for their deletion procedures.
